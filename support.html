Frequently asked questions

What devices does KennelFlow support?

KennelFlow is designed for Apple platforms and is currently focused on iPhone/iPad workflows with Apple-native features.

How does Smart Kennel Scanning work?

Assign an NFC tag or QR code to a kennel/run and scan it to open the related KennelFlow workflow.

Can I import pedigree and report files?

Yes. KennelFlow supports pedigree import plus report/document upload, scanning, viewing, editing, and deletion workflows.

Can employees see each other’s private app data?

KennelFlow is designed around isolated user/tester data and explicit team workflows rather than automatically sharing all personal records.
