Yard Patrol

Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the Yard Patrol Pet Waste Removal iOS app.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

Yard Patrol Pet Waste Removal is a business-management app for pet waste removal services. The app is designed to keep customer, dog, route, job, invoice, expense, photo, and business records on the users device unless the user chooses to export or share them.

Information handled by the app

Users may enter business and customer information such as names, service addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dog information, job notes, invoice details, payment records, expenses, and service photos. These records are stored locally on the device.

Square payments

When a user chooses to collect an electronic invoice payment with Square, Yard Patrol sends the invoice number, payment amount, customer name, and any optional customer email address or phone number needed to prepare the checkout to a secure payment service and Square. Payment card details are entered on Squares hosted checkout and are not stored by Yard Patrol. Yard Patrol may verify the resulting Square order and payment identifier so the invoice can be marked paid.

Photos, backups, and exports

Service photos, JSON backups, and CSV reports remain under the users control. Yard Patrol does not automatically upload these items to a Yard Patrol cloud account. Users may choose to export, share, or store them using services available on their device.

Tracking and advertising

Yard Patrol does not sell personal information and does not use customer information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Service providers

Square processes electronic payments and may process payment-related information under Squares own privacy terms. Hosting and network providers may process limited technical information, such as IP address and request metadata, as necessary to securely operate the payment service.

Data retention and deletion

Records stored in Yard Patrol remain on the device until the user edits or deletes them, restores different backup data, or removes the app and its local data. Payment transaction information maintained by Square is subject to Squares retention requirements and policies.

Security

Yard Patrol uses HTTPS for communication with the payment service. Sensitive payment card information is handled by Squares hosted checkout rather than being stored in the Yard Patrol app.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit Yard Patrol Support.
