Smart Kennel Scanning

Tap the kennel. Open the right care workflow.

KennelFlow combines 33 mm NFC tags and QR codes with kennel records so staff can move faster and document care in the right place.

One tap to the kennel record.

Program an NFC tag for a kennel or run. When scanned, KennelFlow opens the assigned location so the handler can review care, mark duties complete, and access the right records.

Workflow

Designed for speed.

1

Program

Assign the writable NFC tag to the correct kennel/run inside KennelFlow.

2

Tap or Scan

Staff taps the NFC tag or scans the QR code from the Home shortcut.

3

Complete Care

Open the correct care list, mark duties, and save records for that location.
