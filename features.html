Features

Complete kennel management, organized in one place.

KennelFlow is built around the real day-to-day work of managing dogs, litters, kennels, employees, clients, payments, documents, and care.

🐕

Dogs

Photos, AKC and other registries, registration numbers, titles, coat/color, DOB, weight, genetics, OFA/PennHip and more.

🐶

Litters & Puppies

Puppy profiles, registration, vaccines, worming, vet records, photos, sale status, and records.

🩺

Health, Vaccines & X-Ray

Vaccines, wormers, OFA/PennHip, uploaded records, scanned reports, and reminders.

🏠

Kennel Management

Kennel/run assignments, care lists, QR/NFC access, duty tracking, and employee activity.

✓

Daily Duties

Morning, evening, and night checklists that reset while keeping printable history records.

🛏

Boarding

Drop-off, pickup, kennel number, owner, medications, feeding, notes, costs, and balances.

👥

Clients & Employees

Customer records, employee management, team access, and operational activity.

🎯

Training

Hunting, protection, obedience, agility, SAR, narcotics, explosives, dock diving, tracking and more.

💳

Payments

Puppy payments, boarding payments, manual recording, receipts, balances, and supported payment options.

📄

Contracts

Create contracts in-app, upload, scan, photograph, edit, delete, preview, and export.

🧬

Pedigrees

Up to seven generations, scan/import support, historical records, and printable pedigree output.

📊

Expenses & Analytics

Expense categories, daily/weekly/yearly views, spending history, and business tracking.

📎

Reports & Documents

Upload, scan, edit, delete, organize by folders, preview, and share saved reports.

🧪

Breeding & Reproduction

Heat cycles, breeding methods, semen records, units, quality reports, and scanned documents.

🗺

Location Sharing

Launch Apple Maps / Find My-style sharing from KennelFlow for supported workflows.
