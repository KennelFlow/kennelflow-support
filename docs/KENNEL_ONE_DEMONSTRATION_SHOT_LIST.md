# Kennel One — three real demonstration video shot lists

These are recording plans, not finished videos or proof of feature success. Use Build 128 or a later approved build, a dedicated demo account with non-sensitive records, and capture actual app interactions rather than synthetic still-image animations. Record both vertical App Store-compliant footage and 16:9 social crops where feasible.

## Video 1 — All-in-one kennel management (45–60 seconds)

1. Show the green Kennel One K1 app icon and mountain-branded dashboard.
2. Open Dogs; show adding a **fictional demo dog**, editing the profile and returning to its saved record.
3. Open Litters and an existing **test litter** and return to Home.
4. Open More and show the location of boarding, expenses and records.
5. End with clear Kennel One branding and the existing App Store listing link.
6. Do not claim unsupported Android availability, existing real customer counts or demo data as production data.

## Video 2 — Smart QR/NFC kennel system (45–60 seconds)

1. Show Smart tab and Smart Kennel assignments for a **disposable test kennel**.
2. Assign an unused QR/NFC tag with a simulated kennel profile in the test environment.
3. Show the physical QR code being scanned from the app and the correct dog’s care profile opening.
4. Demonstrate feeding, supplements and medication details using fictional examples.
5. If the actual physical NFC tag and an NFC-capable iPhone are available, repeat with NFC. Otherwise explicitly omit or label that segment as unverified.
6. Never show live customer addresses, medication details or credentials.

## Video 3 — Daily care, calendar and cloud workflow (45–60 seconds)

1. Show Today's Care with Morning, Afternoon and Night shifts.
2. Complete one test duty, navigate away, return and confirm its saved status.
3. Open the calendar, tap a day and show that day's scheduled activity list.
4. Log in to a **disposable test** web account and show the corresponding safe shared test record after sync.
5. Make a deliberate conflict in two test clients only if isolated conflict-resolution behavior can be safely demonstrated; never overwrite production customer records.
6. End on Kennel One branding and an App Store call to action.

## Production/approval gates

- Real in-app screen recordings and edits are required; these shot lists are not video deliverables.
- Obtain real owner/tester validation of NFC hardware and subscription purchasing.
- Confirm Apple's App Preview dimensions, encoding and policy requirements before submitting media.
- Keep physical inventory, legacy QR payloads and original purchase identifiers unchanged.
