Privacy at Kennel One

This draft describes the Kennel One app and companion online services. It must be reviewed and approved by the business owner before publication.

Information stored when you use cloud features

Kennel One lets account holders enter and synchronize their business name, name and email, employee identities, kennel and dog records, daily duties, schedules, documents, photos, customer/contact details, contracts, expenses, payment records and other notes they choose to save. Some information may be associated with the account or business that created it.

Employee messaging and account access

When employee cloud features are used, employee names, email addresses, team information, account identifiers and employee messages may be sent to Apple's CloudKit or the business synchronization service. Authentication and access data are used for account sign-in and team features.

Payments and purchases

Payment providers, including Square or Apple, process applicable payments or in-app purchases. Kennel One may keep order identifiers, payment status, amount, customer references and records required for its accounting features. Payment card details handled by payment providers are subject to their policies.

Service providers and purpose

Kennel One uses cloud services to synchronize records across devices, authenticate accounts, support employee collaboration and provide requested features. Providers involved may include Apple (App Store, CloudKit), Render and Neon for service hosting and database storage, and Square for payments. Users should review the providers' privacy policies where relevant.

Account and privacy requests

Authorized users can manage many records from within Kennel One and the connected website. For questions about access, corrections or account/data deletion requests, email kennelflowapp@gmail.com. The response process, retention schedule, backup lifecycle, international transfers and any optional analytics must be verified and finalized before publishing this policy.

Disclosure review

The App Store's privacy summary must reflect information actually collected by the currently available app version and integrated services. This staging draft does not itself update the App Store privacy label.
