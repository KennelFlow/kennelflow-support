Frequently asked questions

How are payments handled?

Electronic invoice payments use Square's secure hosted checkout. Cash, check, and other payments can also be recorded manually.

Does Yard Patrol require an account?

No. Yard Patrol is designed as an owner-operated business management app and does not require an app login.

Where is my Yard Patrol data stored?

Business records are stored on the device. The app also provides JSON backup and restore tools and CSV reporting exports.

Can I manage recurring service?

Yes. Yard Patrol supports weekly, biweekly, one-time, initial cleanup, and inactive service plans with route and job tracking.
